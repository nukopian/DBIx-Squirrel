## Background

Answering 2026-0057's two messages, Ekorn::Pipeline v0.4.3 gives its
iterator `next`, as DBIx::Squirrel's has, and its how-to a section,
"Coming from DBIx::Squirrel", for our readers. Its task asked that our
tests run against v0.4.3, and left raising our floor to us. They
passed on 5.36 and 5.44 against Ekorn-Pipeline's working copy at the
tag. MetaCPAN didn't list v0.4.3 yet when this started.

Read against our code, its section listed `finish` among our
iterator's methods, which ours lacks (it has `reset`, which Ekorn's
lacks), and gave our Tooling Ekorn's tags with `:rows`, where since
2026-0059 they hold lists of our own, and our `:core` holds `is_stop`
and `is_stage` besides.
