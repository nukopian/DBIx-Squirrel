## Background

Ekorn's 2026-0060 fixes `result_original` after a stage that holds
values back. Before it, the core kept one original, the source's for
the value it gave last, and took every stage to be a map, so the
stages after `batch` or `take_last` saw the latest row's original,
whichever value they had. Our statement source's `original` part,
`sub { $context->{row} }`, answers for the row fetched last, so this
reached our queries too.

Now a stage that holds values back says so, `holds => 1` among its
parts, and marks what it gives with the origin of the value it came
from (`kept_origin`, then `$origin->gives(...)`). Our part is called
only when such a stage keeps a value. A stage that doesn't say
`holds` is taken for a map, as before, so our users' own gathers, such
as `batches_of` in DBIx::Squirrel::Gather's synopsis, behave as they
did. `gather`'s message is unchanged.

What our users see change, with the stages we re-export as they are:

- a batch from `batch` has no original, where one filled by a row had
  that row's;
- `take_last`'s value has its row's original, where it had none;
- a value a holding stage gives from its flush has its original, where
  our POD says a flush's values have none; `collect`'s is still
  `undef`.

Ekorn measured it on our queries, 200,000 rows through
`$dbh->iterate` on Perl 5.36: nothing for maps and `take`, which take
its plain loop; about 1% for `count_items`; 4 to 5% for `batch`;
about 26% for `take_last`, which Iain set aside as a debugging tool's.

Our suite passed against Ekorn-Pipeline's working copy at
be949abbab094d7322d201150acc30546ec6cc2e, on Perl 5.36: 37 files and
2,838 assertions, the two subtests the task named included
(`t/06-gather.t`'s "what a flush can ask" and
`t/26-context-in-pipelines.t`'s `collect` flush), as neither reads a
value a stage declaring `holds` gives. No test of ours pins the
originals that change. What does is our text: the Context and Gather
POD on a flush's values, the paragraph on the run's context in
`docs/REFERENCE.md`, and the parts of a gather listed in the POD and
the reference, which gain `holds`.

The range moves to `>= v0.5.0, < v0.6.0` once Ekorn v0.5.0 is on
CPAN; until then, this is planned and tested against Ekorn's
`develop`.

## Decisions

- [x] **D1.** Take `kept_origin` into DBIx::Squirrel::Context and
  Tooling's `:context`? Yes, as Ekorn's own sub, as Iain decided on
  2026-11-27, as recommended: the two `:context` tags stay the same,
  and our users find it beside `result_original`. `t/28-tooling.t`
  needs a name both export to be one sub, as `result_original` is.
  The other option, leaving it out, would have kept our surface
  smaller, as only a stage that holds values back calls it.
- [x] **D2.** Our version: v5.0.0 or v4.3.0? v4.3.0, as Iain decided
  on 2026-11-27, against the recommendation of v5.0.0, which Claude
  then withdrew. Our POD says `result_original` is the row a result is
  being made from: a batch, made from many rows, had the last row's,
  so its `undef` keeps the promise, where before it was broken; and
  `take_last`'s value only gains its row where it had `undef`. So it's
  a fix, and v4.0.0 (`pipe`'s scalar result, 2026-0048), which changed
  behaviour that worked as documented, isn't the precedent. And
  nobody gets it until they install v4.3.0, as v4.2.0's range stops
  below Ekorn v0.5.0.
- [x] **D3.** How much of `holds` our docs explain? A sentence naming
  it among a gather's parts and saying what it does, with a link to
  Ekorn's reference for writing such a stage, as Iain decided on
  2026-11-27, as recommended, so the two documents can't disagree. The
  other option was our own example with `kept_origin` and `gives`.

## The plan

**What:** Ekorn::Pipeline `>= v0.5.0, < v0.6.0`; `kept_origin` in
Context and Tooling's `:context`, as Ekorn's own sub; the POD and the
reference saying what `result_original` gives past stages that hold
values back, and naming `holds`; tests pinning the originals that
changed.

**Why:** Ekorn v0.5.0 keeps 2026-0046's promise past stages that hold
values back, and our text said otherwise.

**Steps:**

1. Ekorn v0.5.0 into the five `@dbix-squirrel-2` libraries, each
   checked; the range in `cpanfile`, and `minil build` for
   `META.json`.
2. `kept_origin` in Context and Tooling; Context's and Gather's POD;
   `docs/REFERENCE.md` and `docs/HOWTO.md`; `scripts/manuals`.
3. A subtest in `t/26-context-in-pipelines.t`: `take_last`'s value
   with its row, a batch with none, and a gather of the user's own
   declaring `holds`, read by `all` and through `|`.
4. The suite on 5.36 and 5.44, against CPAN and Ekorn's working copy;
   `prove`; coverage; the linters.
5. `Changes`, this record, `TODO.md`'s two tasks; a message to Ekorn.

**Done when:** the suite passes against Ekorn v0.5.0, the range takes
it, and Ekorn has our message saying so.
