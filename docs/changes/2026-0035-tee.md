## Background

Ekorn::Pipeline::Stages::Taps has `tee(@stages)`, its 2026-0048,
exported on request, in v0.3.6, and Ekorn's Tooling has it in
`:taps`. DBIx::Squirrel::Stages::Taps imports Ekorn's taps by name, so
`tee` reaches our users only once it's listed there, and in Tooling's
`:taps`. Listing it means requiring Ekorn >= v0.3.6, so it waits for
that release (2026-0034, D2), and is built meanwhile on a branch,
tested against Ekorn's working copy. Until then, `xt/ekorn-tooling.t`
fails in CI once v0.3.6 is on CPAN, saying `tee` is missing.

## The plan

**What:** Stages::Taps exports `tee` on request, as `dump_to`, and in
`:all`; Tooling's `:taps` has it; the `cpanfile` takes Ekorn
`>= v0.3.6, < v0.4.0`; the POD, the reference, the how-to and their
tests say so.

**Why:** a tap Ekorn's users have, ours should, by the same name.

**Done when:** all tests, `xt/` included, pass against Ekorn v0.3.6,
and v3.2.0 is on CPAN.

## What happened

- **Built on the branch,** before Ekorn's v0.3.6, and tested against
  Ekorn's `develop` at 9b206d0b972bb141647f1e123088218f76875c8e: the
  suite, `xt/` included, passes on 5.36.3 and 5.44.0, and `prove -l t`
  on 5.36.3. `t/20-stages-taps.t` runs `tee` on a statement: every row
  passed on past a stop in the branch, `pluck` by name and `as_hash`
  in the branch on array rows, from the run's context, and a death in
  the branch killing the run. The how-to has an example, run by
  `xt/examples.t`.
- **Ekorn v0.3.6 came out on 2026-11-18.** The branch was rebased on
  `develop`, Changes' entry moved above v3.1.1's, and the suite run
  against v0.3.6 from CPAN, on every Perl from 5.36.3 to 5.44.0.
- **Changes from the plan:** none.
- **Released** in v3.2.0, 2026-11-18.
- **What's left:** nothing.
